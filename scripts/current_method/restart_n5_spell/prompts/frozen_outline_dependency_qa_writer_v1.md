# Frozen Outline Dependency QA Writer v1

Write one natural question and canonical answer from the frozen minimal parent
and child evidence. You do not choose evidence and you may not change the frozen
joint result. The question must require the relation between both sides; it must
not ask for a fact stated completely by either side alone and must not reveal the
intermediate reasoning bridge.

Do not output evidence aliases, quotations, distractors, options, explanations,
or answer letters. The local program owns and attaches evidence provenance.
