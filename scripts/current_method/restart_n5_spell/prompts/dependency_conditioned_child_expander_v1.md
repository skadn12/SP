# Dependency-Conditioned Child Expander v1

Create a small child graph continuation that implements the frozen planned child
event exactly. The dependency contract is immutable.

You may add a few surrounding events needed for a natural continuation, but you may
not change the parent operand, subject, planned event, or joint result. Do not state
the joint result. Do not repeat the parent operand or explain its connection to the
new event. Do not generate a question, answer, option, or dialogue.

Every child event must be new. Identify exactly which child event realizes the
planned child event. Keep the continuation minimal enough to verbalize in 4–12
dialogue turns.
