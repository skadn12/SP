Choose concrete, transcript-supported relations for the original three intent slots. A discriminative_variable must state what a question should distinguish, relate, or constrain; a broad topic label alone is insufficient. A relation need not be a binary contrast or a causal transition.

Construct each intent by identifying the specific distinction, checking the transcript support for its claims, selecting the evidence needed to support it, and assigning that evidence to the original chain roles. Do not collect a topic-related passage and merely name its topic. These are construction instructions, not additional output fields: return only the original response schema.

Keep who did what, to whom, in which event, and under which conditions separate when the transcript distinguishes them. Do not join events solely because they involve the same person or subject. Use references that support the stated relation, including context needed to resolve speakers, pronouns, scope, or conditions. Do not fill a chain with contiguous turns just because they are nearby, or add scenes to make it appear complex.

Preserve the status actually supported by the dialogue: considered or discussed; proposed or suggested; planned or intended; decided or confirmed; scheduled or arranged; started or in progress; completed; cancelled; or an observed result. These are distinctions to respect, not a required sequence, new enum, or extra response field. Absence of evidence that an action occurred does not establish that it did not occur. If only a suggestion or intention is supported, do not claim execution, non-execution, success, or failure beyond what the dialogue establishes.

Distinguish event time from the order in which events are mentioned. A later recollection is not automatically a later event or a newly reached state. A future goal, hoped-for benefit, or expected result is not an observed outcome. A question is not an event trigger or cause merely because it precedes another turn. Claim a temporal, causal, or state-transition relation only when supported by the dialogue.

Keep the original meanings of before_state, trigger, and after_state, and the original allowed intent types. The three non-empty, disjoint groups must jointly express the actual relation claimed by the intent. Do not redefine them as arbitrary fact buckets, invent a trigger for a static comparison, or turn a noncausal relation into a causal story to fill the schema. Select a relation that can be represented faithfully in the existing contract; do not change the contract.

For each group, check what its evidence establishes and why it belongs in that role. Do not conflate recalled events, current conditions, and future intentions in a way that changes their status or event relationships. A turn may contain more than one such statement; its inclusion does not make all those statements a single event or state. If the proposed distinction or grouping is unsupported, choose a supported relation while composing the same response. Do not output self-review, extra explanations, bindings, QA, or new fields.
