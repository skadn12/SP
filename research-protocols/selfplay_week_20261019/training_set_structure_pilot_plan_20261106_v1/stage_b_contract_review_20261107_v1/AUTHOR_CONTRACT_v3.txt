GENERAL CONTENT RULES
Use only source-supported facts. Preserve who said or did what, and distinguish reported, planned, suggested, attempted, and completed events. Mention order alone does not establish event order, precedence, or causality. Cite the exact turn reference and exact supporting substring for every fact needed to determine the answer. State the natural information need and exact query scope. Record alternative sufficient evidence paths and unresolved relations rather than claiming a unique minimal proof without support.

An item may use multi-select, complete ordering, or partial ordering. No fixed number of choices, answer labels, task type, or complexity is required. Every choice must be independently decidable from the source. Complete ordering is allowed only if the requested items have one source-supported total order. Partial ordering must define the full queried pair domain and return all and only supported directed relations; do not force unresolved pairs into an order. A locally easy or simple item is acceptable if it is natural and sound. Do not invent facts, bundle unrelated events, change the source, or optimize to a presumed solver score.

ONE-ATTEMPT RULE
Each opportunity permits one author attempt. No repair, rewrite, replacement item, second slot set, retry, fallback, reselection, or replacement opportunity is allowed. Preserve submitted bytes. Any invalid/malformed output or access violation is terminal; do not silently correct it. A terminal opportunity remains in the fixed denominator.

ITEM RECORD
An item record must include: anonymous opportunity code; workflow; source hash; frozen target/slot hash; one item index equal to 1; type; natural-language question; displayed options when applicable; answer key as displayed option identifiers or the complete ordered items/pairs; natural information need; query scope; cognitive task/answer program; event identity when applicable; relation plan; exact evidence and option-by-option evidence/reason audit; alternative sufficient paths; unresolved relations; and any quality concerns. The full schema is in OUTPUT_SCHEMAS_v1.json. Do not include private identity fields or files/paths outside the authorized anonymous packet. No fixed option count or answer cardinality applies.

For partial ordering include every queried pair, classify each as supported in a direction or not established by the source, and cite support for every positive relation. For complete ordering verify every requested item appears exactly once and the order is unique. For multi-select provide an independent source-grounded judgment for every option; do not force distractors or an answer count.
