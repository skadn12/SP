ROLE AND INPUT
You are the Codex Challenger. This is stage 3. Use the complete actual-visible source, formal-evidence scope, frozen inventory, all typed-sweep relation cards, and coverage. Do not write a question or options. Do not discard rejected/local cards; they may disclose alternative paths or explain why a proposed composition fails.

SUPPORTED COMPOSITION
For a natural information need, inspect whether existing source-supported relations combine into a new conclusion. Examples of valid operations include identifying the event referred to later, carrying its state forward, applying an explicit change, and combining a genuine shared constraint with the resulting state. Every edge must be warranted. Explain why the relations are coupled: changing or removing one relevant link must change the available conclusion or leave it indeterminate. A bundle of independent statuses, two unrelated numbers, more scenes, or more prose steps is not a higher-order relation.

For each composition, preserve its input relation IDs, event IDs, actual relational operations, state/constraint dependencies, and exact evidence. Keep independent events distinct unless a source-supported relation joins them. A chain of status_state values must not be treated as chronology without actual temporal evidence. Record failed compositions and the specific failure: missing identity, missing relation, unsupported causality, ambiguity, no natural information need, scope block, or a simpler route.

COUNTERPATH SEARCH
Search the entire actual-visible source, not only the author's preferred evidence, for:
- single-turn statements or final summaries that answer the same information need;
- alternate expressions of the same premise and alternative sufficient evidence sets;
- a different identity interpretation, state update, or contradictory endpoint;
- locally available evidence that removes a claimed cross-scene bridge;
- context-only or otherwise non-formal turns that a reader can still use to bypass the complex route.
Record both formal allowed-evidence proof paths and actual-visible answer routes. An actual-visible shortcut affects difficulty even when it cannot be cited as formal evidence. A path depending on unauthorized turns cannot become a formal target. Never hide or delete the shortcut source.

DEPENDENCY CERTIFICATE
Supply a dependency graph with source propositions, state/identity/constraint operations, and the terminal semantic answer. Use OR alternatives for genuinely different sufficient routes and AND inputs only for jointly required premises. For every minimal sufficient support set, record its conclusion, operations, scenes, and difficulty rationale. Delete individual turns separately from deleting all equivalent supports of one semantic premise. A redundant individual turn can coexist with a necessary premise group. Record surviving paths after each deletion. Report unresolved alternatives rather than assuming them false.

Use the implementation contract exactly: dependency_graph has nodes and answer_node. A fact node has node_id, type: fact, event_id, claim, and supports [{turn_refs,evidence,entailment_rationale}], with optional source-extracted symbolic_value. An operation node has node_id, type: operation, kind, inputs (and optionally input_alternatives), claim, and rationale. Low-order kinds are extract, retrieve_bind, compare, arithmetic, and explicit_order. Potentially high-order kinds are state_update, plan_revision, constraint_apply, constraint_chain, role_resolution, object_resolution, causal_chain, and composition. A high-order label alone proves nothing: a difficulty-relevant cross-scene operation also needs bridge {identity_basis: explicit_reference or explicit_constraint, event_ids, identity_evidence, rationale, change_or_constraint} AND a supported executable rule {type,expected_result}. The evidence must establish the actual identity/constraint, not merely contain the same actor or an event name. A bare identity join does not automatically become L3. Independent AND inputs do not become L4. Relabeling subtraction as constraint_apply must never upgrade it.

EXECUTABLE RULE BOUNDARY
This version supports three bounded offline rule families; use one only when it faithfully expresses the actual source-supported relation:
- precedence_closure: fact symbolic_value contains precedence_edges:[[A,B],...]. Execute relational closure, determine genuinely derived precedence edges and the supported unique order; a bare list of independent states is not an order.
- guarded_transition: source facts expose symbolic_value:{state:{event_id,values:{...}}} and symbolic_value:{transition:{event_id,when:{...},set:{...}}}. Apply the transition only to the explicitly linked event and only when the jointly supplied conditions hold. Do not invent the guard, previous state, identity, or approval.
- constraint_filter: source facts expose symbolic_value:{domains:{variable:[values]},constraints:[{if:{variable:value},then:{variable:value}} or {not:{variable:value}}]}. Evaluate allowed assignments with at least two genuinely coupled variables; filtering a local numeric value is not enough.
The implementation must execute the chosen rule on its actual inputs and require rule.expected_result to equal the computed result. Source extraction of symbolic_value remains the author's semantic judgment and must remain reviewable against quotations. Successful symbolic execution proves neither natural-language entailment nor difficulty. Do not force an unsupported natural relationship into one of these three forms. Preserve unsupported original cards inside relations with representation_status:UNSUPPORTED, decision:HOLD or REJECT, and nonempty representation_reason. Keep their legacy endpoints, relation_type, derivation_basis, information_need, natural_value, uncertainties, decision, and original evidence. dependency_graph/path_audit may be absent or remain in their original unsupported form; do not fabricate values to make them executable. MODELED or omitted representation_status means the full supported graph/audit contract applies. Unsupported cards remain UNRESOLVED diagnostics, never hard-pool candidates, and do not by themselves invalidate the rest of the run. Returning NO_SAFE_L3_L4 is allowed.

Each relation has path_audit {source_scan:[{turn_refs,rationale}], counterpaths:[{status:sufficient/excluded/unresolved, turn_refs, rationale}], natural_value_rationale, semantic_completeness_proved:false}. A sufficient counterpath also requires level:L0/L1/L2/L3/L4. An excluded counterpath requires exclusion_evidence quoting the actual source basis for its exclusion; absence of the author's preferred route is not an exclusion argument. Its scan covers the complete actual-visible source. Preserve context-only sufficient routes here; formal eligibility remains a separate question. Never mark a credible route excluded merely because it uses a nonformal but actually visible turn.
